BUILT FOR THE WORK BETWEEN A PAYMENT AND A SETTLED WALLET.

One merchant. Two networks. A workflow your team can follow from the first address to the final sweep.
/ 01 — THE WORKFLOW

A payment flow you can trace.

Connect your business system once, then follow each payment through its operating stages.

  1. 01

    Create a payment

    Your business system creates a payment session and receives a dedicated checkout URL and deposit address.

  2. 02

    Track the deposit

    Reflow watches the selected network, updates the checkout status, and sends a signed webhook to your system.

  3. 03

    Sweep to your main wallet

    Move funds from deposit addresses with operator-initiated or threshold-based sweeps.

/ 04 — DEPLOYMENT MODEL

Run it on your infrastructure.

Reflow is designed as a single-tenant deployment. Address derivation and transaction signing run in the chain worker, separate from the web application. Your team manages the server, wallet configuration, and operating policy.
